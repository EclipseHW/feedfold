# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Echovale is for a small household using a self-hosted RSS reader over a private tailnet. Each person has a separate account, subscriptions, rules, and reading state. Reading happens in focused sessions: scanning a queue, opening an article, moving through it by keyboard, and deliberately preserving anything worth returning to.

## Product Purpose

Echovale is a fast, dependable home for subscribed feeds. It imports and exports OPML, organizes feeds into folders, removes predictable noise with rules, extracts full article text, and keeps reading state explicit. Success means the entire daily reading loop can stay inside the app without social features, discovery surfaces, or routine trips to original websites.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, focused, dependable. The interface should feel calm during long reading sessions, compact while scanning, and generous once an article is open. Its language is direct and factual.

## Anti-references

- Inoreader's shifting product tiers and feature boundaries.
- NewsBlur's heavy, crowded, and unstable experience.
- Social feeds, recommendation streams, engagement mechanics, and dashboard decoration.
- Reader interfaces that hide state, interrupt keyboard flow, or require the original website for normal reading.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the reader in flow. Every frequent action has a predictable keyboard path and a visible equivalent.
2. Put content first. Scanning is compact; the active article is spacious, readable, and free of unrelated controls.
3. Make state obvious. Unread, read, starred, active, refreshing, and failed states are never ambiguous.
4. Stay lean and dependable. Prefer a small number of durable concepts over optional subsystems or clever abstractions.
5. Surface operational truth. Feed failures, last attempts, and last successful refreshes are visible and actionable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, browser zoom up to 200%, and status cues that do not rely on color alone. Single-key shortcuts must pause in editable controls and can be disabled. Article font size is adjustable and persistent.
